## Screenshot 5 Sentence Exercise

Source: [Apply dual forms in sentences](dual-forms-screenshots/2026-10-20-05-sentence-exercise.png).
Instruction: `Aqleb il-kliem skur fl-għadd imtenni.`
English: Change the words in bold into the dual form.

The user's requirement is to practise applying the form to highlighted words
in context, not just memorising isolated word pairs. The slide specifically asks
for the dual, not the ordinary plural. Candidate targets below are an editorial
answer draft, not a teacher-supplied answer key or verified teaching bank.

| Source sentence with highlighted target | Candidate dual target | English target meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| L-**għajn** tat-tifla minfuħa. | l-għajnejn | the two eyes |
| Infaqt **elf** ewro meta sifirt. | elfejn | two thousand |
| Hemm bżonn li taqra **siegħa** kuljum. | sagħtejn | two hours |
| Waqajt u weġġajt l-**irkoppa**. | l-irkopptejn | the two knees |
| Kiser **siequ** u għamluhielu fil-ġibs. | saqajh | his two feet/legs |
| Il-**widna** tal-borma nkisret. | il-widnejn | the two handles of the pot |
| Nixtieq immur btala ta’ **xahar**. | xahrejn | two months |
| Ġarr kollox fuq l-**ispalla**. | l-ispallejn | the two shoulders |

### Requirements for Later Implementation

- Preserve full sentences and highlight exactly the original target.
- First practise only the highlighted form; separately practise complete-sentence
  agreement so the learner understands which task is being graded.
- Preserve articles, prepositions and possessive meaning. In particular, `siequ`
  must keep the meaning "his" rather than become unpossessed `saqajn`.
- Explain contextual meanings: `widna` here means a pot handle; `sieq` may be
  rendered foot or leg depending on context.
- Review agreement in `minfuħa`, `nkisret` and the object pronoun in
  `għamluhielu` before publishing full transformed sentences. Do not mechanically
  replace nouns and leave incompatible agreement behind.
- Use typed `questionAnswer` records, with separate prompts and answers,
  stable question IDs and the shared feedback/review APIs.

Supporting usage consulted on 2026-10-20: the University of Malta
[Maltese specimen paper](https://www.um.edu.mt/media/um/docs/directorates/matsec/markingschemesandsba/specimenpapers/sec/SpecimenPaper-SEC22Malti2025%281%29.pdf)
contains `saqajh` and `għajnejn`; the European Medicines Agency's
[Maltese document](https://www.ema.europa.eu/mt/documents/product-information/unituxin-epar-product-information_mt.pdf)
contains `sagħtejn` and `widnejn`. These attestations do not verify the complete
transformed sentences or the entire draft answer key.
