Sound, broken and dual plurals

Sound pluralAn ending is added or replaced. The stem may also change.

flokk → flokkijiet · festa → festi · wejter → wejters

Broken pluralThe internal pattern changes.

kelb → klieb · skola → skejjel · għalqa → għelieqi

DualSome nouns have a special form for two.

sena → sentejn · ġimgħa → ġimagħtejn

Some nouns have both kinds of plural. Bankijiet refers to benches and banek to financial banks.
