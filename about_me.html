Speaking Route

Build the Answer in Three Passes

1. IdentityName, age, origin, and where you live.
2. Everyday LifeWork, duties, family, and children.
3. Personal DetailHobbies, routines, and one extra weekend detail.
Safe order: Jisimni... → Jien minn... → Noqgħod... → Naħdem... → Għandi... → Inħobb...

Story Ladder

Grow One Introduction Instead of Memorising Three

Start with the first model. Add the next layer only when the shorter version feels automatic.

30 seconds: core facts

Bonġu.Hello.
Jisimni Alex u għandi ħamsa u tletin sena.My name is Alex and I am thirty-five years old.
Jien mill-Italja, imma noqgħod il-Mosta.I am from Italy, but I live in Mosta.
Naħdem bħala għalliem.I work as a teacher.

60 seconds: family and hobbies

Bonġu. Jisimni Alex u għandi ħamsa u tletin sena.Hello. My name is Alex and I am thirty-five years old.
Jien mill-Italja, imma ilni noqgħod f'Malta għal ħames snin.I am from Italy, but I have lived in Malta for five years.
Noqgħod il-Mosta ma' marti u maż-żewġt itfal tagħna.I live in Mosta with my wife and our two children.
Naħdem bħala għalliem.I work as a teacher.
Fil-ħin liberu tiegħi nħobb naqra u nimxi ħdejn il-baħar.In my free time I like reading and walking by the sea.

Two minutes: connected detail

Bonġu. Jisimni Alex u għandi ħamsa u tletin sena.Hello. My name is Alex and I am thirty-five years old.
Jien mill-Italja, imma ilni noqgħod f'Malta għal ħames snin.I am from Italy, but I have lived in Malta for five years.
Bħalissa noqgħod il-Mosta.At present I live in Mosta.
Naħdem bħala għalliem.I work as a teacher.
Fuq ix-xogħol ngħallem il-Malti u nipprepara l-lezzjonijiet.At work I teach Maltese and prepare lessons.
Jien miżżewweġ; marti jisimha Sara, u għandna żewġt itfal.I am married; my wife's name is Sara, and we have two children.
Normalment nieklu flimkien filgħaxija.We normally eat together in the evening.
Fil-ħin liberu tiegħi nħobb naqra u nimxi ħdejn il-baħar.In my free time I like reading and walking by the sea.
Fi tmiem il-ġimgħa niltaqa' mal-ħbieb jew nqatta' l-ħin mal-familja tiegħi.At the weekend I meet friends or spend time with my family.
