Homonyms and similar spellings

Dar can mean “house” or “he turned”. In f'dar it names a place. After a subject such as Pawlu, it can be the verb. The capital letter and article help distinguish il-Ħadd (“Sunday”) from ħadd (“anyone”).

Compare words that sound alike

Look closely at the letters in daq and daqq, and at the apostrophe in tefa and tefa'.

Synonyms

Synonyms share a meaning in a particular context. Tqil means “heavy” for a bag and can mean “difficult” for an exam. When you replace a noun, check its article as well.
