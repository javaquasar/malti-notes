Topic 2

Sport and healthy habits

Name sports, agree descriptive adjectives, and use ipprattika and xorob.

Remember the pattern

Choose the form for the subject: jien nipprattika, aħna nipprattikaw. Commands address one person (ixrob) or a group (ixorbu). Adjectives also change for gender and number.

Topic 3

Days, months and dates

Recall weekdays and months, read numbers 21–31, and describe a weekly plan.

Remember the pattern

For numbers 21–29, say the unit before u għoxrin; 30 is tletin and 31 is wieħed u tletin. Date phrases use forms such as fit-tlieta ta' Mejju. Keep the date pattern separate from counting objects.

Topic 4

Festivals and preferences

Recognise festa vocabulary, describe a local event, and choose jogħġobni, togħġobni or jogħġbuni.

Remember the pattern

The thing you like controls the verb form: masculine singular jogħġobni, feminine singular togħġobni, plural jogħġbuni. The ending -ni refers to me; it does not tell you the gender of the thing liked.

Topic 5

People, possession and jobs

Describe appearance, identify whose something is, and connect a job with its action.

Remember the pattern

The possessive identifies the owner: tiegħu means his, tagħha means her. Use the form for the owner, not for the gender of the object. Compare a male worker's verb with its female form, for example jgħallem / tgħallem.

Topic 6

Ambitions and hobbies

Remember the pattern

Topic 7

My town and future plans

Locate places with prepositions, describe your town, and make plans with se.

Remember the pattern

A place preposition shows where something is. For a future plan, place se before the verb form for the subject: se mmur, se tmur, se jmorru. Do not use the same verb form for every person.
