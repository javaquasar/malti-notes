Role Play Ideas

Customer

Ask for the menu, order one dish and one drink, then ask for the bill.

Il-menu, jekk jogħġbok. / Nixtieq... / Il-kont, jekk jogħġbok.

Waiter / waitress

Offer the menu, ask what the customer would like, and explain how they can pay.

X'tixtieq tiekol? / Trid tħallas bil-karta jew bi flus kontanti?

Wrong vs Right

Wrong

Wrong: Jien tixtieq pizza.

Wrong person and verb form. After jien, the verb should be nixtieq.

Right

Better: Jien nixtieq pizza.

I would like pizza.
