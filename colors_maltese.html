Shades and Useful Words

Shade Words

  • skur = dark
  • ċar = light / pale
  • qawwi = strong / bright
  • ikkulurit = colourful

Useful Combinations

  • blu skur = dark blue
  • aħdar ċar = light green
  • aħmar qawwi = bright red
  • kamra ikkulurita = colourful room

Wrong vs Right

Common Mistakes

Wrong

Il-karozza huwa aħmar.Wrong agreement with a feminine noun.
Jien nixtieq blu qmis.Wrong word order.
Il-bieb blu skura.Wrong shade agreement.
Din aħdar borża.Wrong word order and agreement.

Right

Il-karozza ħamra.The car is red.
Nixtieq qmis blu.I would like a blue shirt.
Il-bieb blu skur.The door is dark blue.
Din borża ħadra.This is a green bag.
